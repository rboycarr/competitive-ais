## Objective
An admin can grant access to the records about one competitor instead of all records (docs/11 P1-20a, docs/02 §3.2, docs/07 §3, ADR-0005; PO decision 2026-11-01). `access_grants.competitor_id` is a typed FK with the widened scope CHECK. `AccessScope` now applies all-of semantics over competitors: a record is covered when every competitor on it is covered at its level and it has no market scope (market grants follow P1-23); an unscoped restricted record still needs an `all` grant. Identity reaches competitors only through Registry's `CompetitorLookup` contract. The authorization matrix gains competitor-grant variants and record shapes; a deliberate mutation was caught by all its paths.

## Review focus
1. Predicate: do `GrantSet::covers()` and the SQL in `GrantAccessScope::applyTo()` implement docs/07 §3 exactly and agree with each other (all-of, restricted needs an explicit grant, markets, expired/revoked/team grants, inactive users)? Can any SQL shape widen results (empty covered set, OR precedence, `whereDoesntHave` with a constraint)?
2. Grant creation: can an admin grant a competitor they cannot see, combine scope and target inconsistently, or bypass the CHECK? Rollback safety of the migration.
3. Matrix: is the oracle independent of the implementation, and do the variants exercise the new branches?
4. Exposure: competitor names on the grants page for competitors the admin cannot see.
5. Module boundaries: Identity ↔ Registry contracts only.
