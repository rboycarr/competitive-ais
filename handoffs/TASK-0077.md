- `source_policy_id` is recorded;

PO decision O12 still applies: evidence may be uploaded without a registered source ("unregistered source").

## Changes
- **Migration `2026_11_22_000100_add_source_policy_to_evidence_assets`:**
  - `source_id` (FK sources, RESTRICT) and `source_policy_id`.
  - `source_policy_decision` is generated (stored) as `'approved'` whenever a policy is recorded.
  - The composite FK (source_policy_id, source_id, source_policy_decision) → source_policies (id, source_id, decision) uses `source_policies_id_source_decision_unique`.
  - CHECK: both columns or neither.
  - `down()` is refused while any asset names a source.
- **Sources contract `SourceLookup`** (bound in `SourcesServiceProvider`, implemented by `EloquentSourceLookup`). Each method filters by `source.view` and `Source::visibleTo` and returns `SourcePolicySummary`:
  - `usableBy`: approved sources with a policy in force;
  - `forCapture(viewer, ulid, lock)`: optionally a `sharedLock` on the source row;
  - `recorded(viewer, sourceId, policyId)`: the policy must be an approval of that source.
- **`EvidenceUploadService`:**
  - An optional `source` (ULID; malformed values are refused).
  - Checked before the object is written, then again inside the transaction with the share lock. The source ID and the policy in force at that moment are written with the asset.
  - A source that is hidden or unknown gives "Choose a source you can see."; one that is not approved gives a distinct message.
  - `evidence.uploaded` gains `source_policy` (id).
- **Controller and UI:**
  - The upload form offers `usableBy` sources to uploaders.
  - `show` returns `asset.source`:
    - null (shown as "Unregistered source");
    - `{visible: false}` when the viewer cannot see the source;
    - otherwise the ULID, name, current status, policy version and decided_at.

## Review focus
1. **Lock and consistency.** Policy decisions and lifecycle changes lock the source row FOR UPDATE. Does the upload's FOR SHARE read inside its transaction guarantee the recorded policy was in force when the upload committed? Consider:
   - REPEATABLE READ snapshots;
   - the earlier non-locking read;
   - the FK checks' implicit shared locks;
   - deadlock risk with `SourcePolicyService`/`SourceLifecycleService`.
2. **Database rules.** Can any write by the app account record:
   - a policy of another source;
   - a rejected or needs_info decision;
   - a source without a policy?

   The columns are not yet immutable after insert (deferred to P1-52; the app writes them only in the upload). Is that acceptable for this slice?
3. **Disclosure.**
   - The placeholder for a hidden source reveals only that one was recorded. Is that acceptable, given the provenance placeholder in P1-45?
   - Do the error messages let an uploader distinguish a hidden source from an unknown one?
   - Does the show page leak the name or status of a source the viewer cannot see?
4. **Module boundaries.** Evidence uses only `Modules\Sources\Contracts`. Is the contract shape right, given that docs/03 named `SourceRegistry::isApprovedFor`?
5. **Tests.** Does `test_a_suspension_committed_after_the_first_check_is_honoured_in_the_transaction` prove the in-transaction re-check? What is not covered?
6. Anything deferred that should not be: retention from the policy goes to P1-38; immutability to P1-52.
