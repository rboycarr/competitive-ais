## Objective
P1-38: "As the platform, duplicate uploads and job retries cannot corrupt shared blobs". Acceptance (docs/11):
- atomic blob insert-or-reload; monotonic scan status;
- race and failure-injection tests;
- a quarantine sweep for objects without an asset (TASK-0064 #2);
- before any blob deletion: recompute the hold from the assets under lock and check the object version's legal hold, never `evidence_blobs.legal_hold` alone; reconcile database and object holds first (TASK-0074);
- per-asset `retention_until` from the `retention_days` of the source policy recorded at capture (P1-37), the default (O33) otherwise.

PO decisions this story (2026-11-05):
- **O38:** the deletion safeguards now; the deleting job, and the database permission it needs, come with the story that adds withdrawal or retention expiry. Today no blob can become unreferenced, and the app account cannot delete blob rows.
- **O39:** retention days count from the upload, not the entered capture time.

Insert-or-reload, the per-hash named lock and monotonic results already shipped in P1-32b. This story adds tests for them under real concurrency and failure injection.

## Changes
- **Migration `2026_11_23_000100_add_retention_to_evidence_assets`:**
  - `retention_until` DATE NOT NULL, backfilled as `DATE(created_at)` plus the recorded policy's `retention_days`, or the config default.
  - CHECK `retention_until >= CAST(created_at AS DATE)`.
  - `down()` drops the derived column; re-applying derives it again.
- **Config:** `evidence.blob_retention_days` is renamed `default_retention_days`. New `quarantine_orphan_minutes` (60).
- **Sources contract:** `SourcePolicySummary` gains `policyRetentionDays`, defaulting to null.
- **`EvidenceUploadService`:** sets `retention_until` inside the transaction, from the policy read under the share lock (P1-37).
- **`EvidenceScanService`:**
  - A new blob's object is locked to `objectRetainUntil(asset date)`: the start of the following day, UTC.
  - `decide()` locks the asset, then the blob (the hold service's order). It links the asset, then calls `EvidenceBlobReconciler::extendToAssets()`, which pushes the stored version first and then updates the row. Extensions only; a failure rolls the decision back and the job retries.
- **`LockedObjects`:**
  - `extendRetention()`: governance mode, never `BypassGovernanceRetention`.
  - `state()`: reads the version's hold and retention. "No lock" codes read as off/none; any other error is raised.
- **`EvidenceBlobReconciler`:**
  - `reconcile()`: under the blob row lock, hold is the OR of the assets and retention is the MAX, applied to the record and pushed to the stored version.
  - An object held while no asset holds it is reported, and released only on `--release-unheld`.
  - Repairs are audited as `evidence.blob_reconciled`.
  - `deletable()`: must run inside a transaction. It reconciles first, then requires no references, the version not held, and both retentions past.
- **Commands:** `evidence:reconcile-blobs` (daily at 02:30) and `evidence:sweep-quarantine` (hourly; orphans are `quarantine/<ULID>` keys older than 60 minutes with no asset holding the key).

## Review focus
1. **Lock order and consistency.**
   - The paths are: scan `decide()` (asset, then blob), `EvidenceHoldService` (asset, then blob) and the reconciler (blob only, then plain reads of assets).
   - Can a reconciliation read stale asset holds or links under REPEATABLE READ?
   - Is there any deadlock or lost-update path?
2. **Store and record ordering.**
   - The object retention is extended before the row; the object hold is set before commit.
   - Can the records ever claim more protection than the store holds, or can a store call shorten or release protection without the operator?
   - Is `objectRetainUntil` (the start of the day after the last day) right?
3. **`deletable()`.** Is it sufficient as the gate for a future deleting job?
   - It depends on the transaction-level check.
   - Assets currently reference a blob only while `available`. A future withdrawal that keeps the reference would still block deletion. Is that the right default?
4. **Quarantine sweep.**
   - Could it delete an object an upload is still committing, or one a scan still needs?
   - Is the 60-minute age safe given the upload's share-lock wait?
   - Is the handling of unexpected keys right?
5. **Migration.** Backfill correctness (the policy join), the NOT NULL and CHECK, and rollback safety.
6. **Tests.**
   - Do `EvidenceBlobRaceTest`, `EvidenceRetentionTest` (a stateful fake store), `EvidenceQuarantineSweepTest` and `PrivateObjectStorageTest` prove the acceptance?
   - What is not covered?
7. **Store answers.** SeaweedFS answered `NoSuchObjectLegalHold` for a version never held. Treating it, and `NoSuchObjectRetention`, as "none" is deliberate. Any risk of reading a missing version as unlocked?
