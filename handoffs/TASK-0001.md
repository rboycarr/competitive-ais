## Review focus (priority order)
1. **Authorization (ADR-0005, `docs/07` §3):** can the ABAC predicate leak data? Gaps between list, single-record, search, download, and AI paths? Is "404 for hidden records" consistently achievable? Is the role → permission matrix coherent with segregation of duties?
2. **Schema (`docs/02`):** missing constraints, wrong FK delete behavior, NULL-in-UNIQUE pitfalls, index coverage for the listed query paths, the append-only-by-grants approach, and the IP-retention exception.
3. **Evidence integrity (ADR-0007):** quarantine → scan → hash → content-addressed storage. Race conditions, dedupe edge cases (same bytes, different classification or source), object-lock interplay with retention and legal hold.
4. **Audit design (ADR-0006):** per-row hash + Merkle checkpoints vs a hash chain. Is tamper evidence adequate? Can transactions that roll back leave gaps that look like tampering?
5. **Intelligence state machine (`docs/03`):** missing transitions, versioning and stale-approval risks, SoD bypasses.
6. **AI/voice designs (`docs/05`, `docs/06`):** prompt-injection and confirmation-bypass paths, even though implementation is Phase 4/5.
7. **Plan realism (`docs/11`):** sequencing risks, missing stories, and under-estimated items in Phase 1.
8. **Consistency:** contradictions between documents, or with `CLAUDE.md` and `skills/`.
