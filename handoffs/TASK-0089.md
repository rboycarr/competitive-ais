## Design calls for review
1. "Queue owner" is treated as the director (`intelligence.publish`). Reviewers do not take items themselves.
2. Reassignment of an in-review item returns it to submitted.
3. The reviewer stays assigned after changes are requested; resubmitting restarts the age.
4. Approval also needs supporting evidence the reviewer can see (stricter than the submit guard).
5. The review history, including rationales, is shown to anyone who can see the item.
6. Review rows on assign use `reviewer_id` for the director and `assignee_id` for the reviewer.

## Review focus
1. **Segregation of duties.** Can anyone other than the assigned reviewer decide, or the author or owner review their own item, through any path (HTTP, service, stale page, races with reassignment)?
2. **Integrity.** Is the content hash canonical and complete enough to bind P1-44's publish check? Are review rows really append-only (grants and CHECKs)?
3. **Visibility.** Queue and item page leak nothing about hidden items. The 404 comes before any validation or permission answer for users with `intelligence.view`.
4. **Concurrency.** Lock order: item, then user row share lock (assign) or evidence share locks (approve). Any deadlock against linking, holds or role changes?
5. **Tests.** What is missing from `VerificationQueueTest` and the race test?
