## Design calls for review
1. The owner cannot publish either, in addition to the author and approver.
2. The Director rule names the `ci_director` role. A custom role holding `intelligence.publish` can publish only internal or public, non-high-impact items.
3. Publishing re-checks that the cited evidence is still available. Evidence withdrawn after approval blocks publication (P1-48 will flag published items).
4. A replacement draft copies the fields and subjects but not the claims; claims and their evidence are cited and reviewed afresh.
5. Retracted and superseded items stay readable, with a banner.
6. Several replacement drafts may exist. The first one published supersedes the original; the others are refused at publish time.

## Review focus
1. **Publish invariant.** Can anything be published without a current-version approval whose hash matches, or by the author, owner or approver? Check stale pages, content changed through any path, and races.
2. **Locking.** Two-row locks in ID order (publish with its original, retract with its replacement) against each other and against the item-then-evidence order used elsewhere. Any deadlock or lost update?
3. **Visibility.** Retraction and supersession links shown only when visible. Replacement options. 404 before anything for hidden items.
4. **Integrity.** Retractions are append-only and unique. `supersedes_id` is never changeable.
5. **Tests.** What is missing?
