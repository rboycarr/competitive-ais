## Changes
- **Migration** `modules/Intelligence/database/migrations/2026_11_25_000100_create_claim_evidence_links_table.php`:
  - `claim_evidence_links` as in docs/02 §3.6: FK CASCADE to the claim, RESTRICT to the evidence asset, `linked_by`, DATETIME(6) `created_at`, UNIQUE(claim, asset, relation);
  - CHECKs on the relation and non-blank locator and excerpt;
  - grant class `mutable` (links are added and removed while the item is editable);
  - `down()` refuses while any link exists.
- **Evidence contract** `modules/Evidence/src/Contracts/EvidenceLinking.php` (implementation `Lookup/EloquentEvidenceLinking.php`, bound in `EvidenceServiceProvider::register`):
  - `linkable` (the picker), `lockLinkable` (share lock; available and seen), `summaries`, `recordLink` (`linked` / `unlinked` custody events);
  - visibility as on the evidence pages: `evidence.view`, then `EvidenceAsset::seenBy`.
- **Sources contract:** `SourceLookup::summaries` and `SourceSummary`, carrying `defaultReliability`.
- **`IntelligenceClaimService::link` / `unlink`**, through the same `inItem` path as the other claim changes:
  - `intelligence.create`; item seen; under the item's row lock: seen again, editable, author or owner, version read;
  - every change raises the version;
  - link: an active claim; at most 20 links; asset locked in share mode, seen and `available`; each (asset, relation) once; locator single-line (whitespace collapsed, ≤ 255); excerpt multi-line (≤ 2,000);
  - unlink: only a link whose asset the actor can see (others are "not found").
  - Audit `intelligence.evidence_linked` / `evidence_unlinked`: claim ULID, evidence ULID, relation, version; classification is the higher of the item's and the asset's. Never the locator or excerpt.
  - Lock order: the item, then the asset.
- **Item page:**
  - each claim's citations, described only when the viewer can see the asset (title, status, relation, locator, excerpt, source name and default reliability, or "source hidden"); others counted;
  - a picker (title search as a partial reload) and a cite form; the relation is never defaulted;
  - "Stop citing".
- **Not in this story:**
  - inherited scopes on link (P1-36b, next);
  - the evidence page's list of claims citing it (P1-45, provenance);
  - re-review when cited evidence is withdrawn (docs/03, with withdrawal).

## Review focus
1. **Authorization and leakage.**
   - Can a citation's locator, excerpt, title, ULID or source name reach a viewer who cannot see the asset, through the page props, the picker, the audit trail or errors?
   - Is `evidence.view` enforced everywhere?
   - Unlink of a hidden asset.
2. **Concurrency.**
   - The share lock on the asset under the item lock.
   - The duplicate check under the item lock.
   - Version checks: can a link change content without raising the version, or after the item left `draft` / `changes_requested`?
3. **Integrity.**
   - Is the custody trail written in the same transaction?
   - Is `mutable` the right grant class for links, given that P1-43 will hash the claims and links at a version?
   - Should the excerpt be checked against the asset in any way?
4. **Tests.** Do `ClaimEvidenceLinkTest` and `IntelligenceRaceTest` (moves and links from one version; create versus archive in both orders; rollback refusal with committed rows) prove the acceptance and the deferred items? What is missing?
