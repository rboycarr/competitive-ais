## Objective
P1-35: "As a director, I place evidence on legal hold". Acceptance: a hold flag plus a custody event; retention cannot expire held items; the blob's hold is the OR of its assets' holds (ADR-0007).

PO decisions, 2026-11-05:
- **O35:** a reason is required to set and to release a hold.
- **O36:** only available evidence can be held.

## Changes
- **Migration `2026_11_20_000100_add_legal_hold_to_evidence_assets`:**
  - adds `evidence_assets.legal_hold` (default false);
  - adds `evidence_assets_hold_check` (`legal_hold = 0 OR status = 'available'`);
  - `down()` is refused while any asset is held.
- **`LockedObjects::legalHold()`:** `PutObjectLegalHold` ON/OFF on the stored version.
  - The version ID is required.
  - A non-S3 disk is skipped, in tests only.
- **`EvidenceHoldService::change()`:**
  - Checks evidence.hold and visibility (`seenBy`; hidden = 404), then validates the reason: required, at most 1000 characters, valid UTF-8, no control characters except tab, CR and LF.
  - In one transaction it locks the asset FOR UPDATE, then the blob FOR UPDATE, and refuses an asset that isn't available or is already in the requested state.
  - It updates the asset, recomputes the blob's hold as the OR over its assets, and saves the blob if that changed.
  - Writes custody `hold_set`/`hold_released` (details: the reason) and audit `evidence.hold_set`/`hold_released` (context: `blob_hold` on/off).
- **Object push ordering:**
  - **Blob hold turns on:** pushed ON inside the transaction, so a store failure rolls everything back.
  - **Blob hold turns off:** after the commit, `pushBlobHold()` pushes the blob's current state under its row lock. A hold set meanwhile on a sibling asset is pushed as ON, never overwritten by a stale OFF. If that push fails, the error is logged and reported; the object stays held.
- **Controller, route and provider:**
  - POST `/evidence/{asset}/hold` (`can:evidence.hold`), validates `hold` (boolean) and `reason`.
  - `show` adds `asset.legalHold`, `canHold` (available + evidence.hold) and `reason` on hold custody events.
  - Two new audit actions are registered.
- **UI (`Show.vue`):** a legal-hold notice, a reason plus place/release form for holders, and reasons in the custody list (as text).

## Review focus
1. **Under-holding:** can the object ever be OFF while any referencing asset is held? Consider:
   - concurrent set and release on two assets sharing a blob;
   - the post-commit push;
   - a crash between commit and push;
   - a store error on either side.
2. **Locking:** the lock order is asset then blob, and `pushBlobHold` locks only the blob. Any deadlock, or a lost update?
3. **Authorization and validation:** evidence.hold together with `seenBy` (directors see unavailable assets, which are refused with a validation error), the reason rules, and the CHECK constraint interplay with future status changes (a withdraw must release first).
4. **Acceptance:** "Retention cannot expire held items" is met by the S3 legal hold on the version, plus `evidence_blobs.legal_hold` for the future deletion job (P1-38). There are no per-asset retention dates yet (P1-37/P1-38). Is that a gap for this story?
5. **Data minimisation:** the reason lives in the custody details (visible to those who see the asset), never in the audit context.
