## Objective
Verify the disposition in `handoffs/TASK-0082-review.md` of the high finding: the quarantine sweep could delete the object of an upload stalled between its write and its commit.

The fix is an ownership lock:
- The upload takes the MySQL named lock `evidence-upload:<ULID>` before writing its object.
- It releases the lock after its asset commits (`afterCommit`), or after cleaning up a failed upload.
- The sweep takes that lock with a zero timeout and skips a key whose lock is held ("in use by an upload").
- Holding the lock, the sweep re-checks for an asset before deleting.

`retention_until` immutability is added to P1-52.

## Review focus
1. Is the ownership protocol sound? Consider:
   - an upload inside an outer transaction (`afterCommit` waits for the outermost commit; a rolled-back outer transaction leaves the lock until the connection closes);
   - connection loss or a process crash;
   - lock-name length;
   - the scan path, whose asset holds the key while quarantined;
   - the sweep's re-check.
2. Does `test_a_sweep_during_a_stalled_upload_leaves_its_object` prove the race fix? (Removing the sweep's lock check makes it fail.)
3. Any new issue in the fix commit.
