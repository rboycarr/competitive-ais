## Objective
P1-48: "As a reader, I know when published intelligence depends on withdrawn or corrupted evidence". Acceptance (docs/11): evidence events flag the items citing it for re-review; a banner is shown; nothing is retracted automatically.

Two PO decisions (2026-11-07, docs/11):
- **O41.** Nothing could withdraw evidence or re-check its integrity, so both triggers are built now, minimally.
- **O42.** The item keeps its status and gets a flag, instead of moving to in_review as the docs/03 table said. Moving a published item to in_review would hide it from its readers.

## Changes
- **Evidence: withdrawal** (`EvidenceWithdrawalService`, `POST /evidence/{asset}/withdraw`):
  - Who and what: `evidence.hold` (no new permission); an asset the actor sees (404 otherwise); `available` and not on legal hold.
  - Reason: as for holds (`EvidenceHoldService::reason`, now public static). It is kept in custody, not in the audit context.
  - Locks: asset `FOR UPDATE`, then blob `FOR UPDATE`.
  - Effect: status becomes `withdrawn` and `evidence_blob_id` becomes NULL (`evidence_assets_blob_check`). The blob and its object are kept.
  - Records, in one transaction: a `withdrawn` custody event (reason and SHA-256), the `evidence.withdrawn` audit event (SHA-256) and an outbox event `evidence.withdrawn` (`{evidence_ulid}`).
- **Evidence: integrity re-hash** (`EvidenceIntegrityService::verify`; `evidence:verify-integrity --limit=50`, daily 03:15):
  - Reading: the stored version is read outside any transaction through the new `LockedObjects::readVersion`. A missing object or version reads as null; any other store error is raised.
  - Then: assets referencing the blob are locked `FOR UPDATE` in ID order, then the blob; assets that took the blob in the meantime are locked after it.
  - Match: `last_verified_at` is set.
  - Mismatch or missing: the blob is marked `mismatch` for good. Each available asset on it gets custody `integrity_failed` (system), the `evidence.integrity_failed` audit event (result, SHA-256) and an outbox event.
  - Marked blobs are skipped later.
- **`EloquentEvidenceLinking`:** "available" now also requires the blob's `integrity_status = 'ok'` in `available`, `availableSeen`, `linkable` and `lockLinkable`. With `$lock`, the blobs are share-locked after the assets (`intact()`).
- **Intelligence:**
  - Migration `2026_11_28_000100`:
    - `rereview_flags` (append-only; unique item+asset+cause; cause CHECK);
    - `intelligence_items.rereview_since`;
    - the `verification_reviews` decision CHECK gains `clear_rereview`, which requires a rationale.
  - `FlagCitingItems` (the `OutboxHandler` for both events, the first outbox consumer):
    - targets approved or published items whose non-withdrawn claims cite the asset, any relation;
    - locks the items in ID order and re-checks their status;
    - adds one flag per item, asset and cause, skipping existing ones;
    - sets `rereview_since` if null;
    - audits `intelligence.rereview_flagged` as the system.
  - `IntelligenceWorkflowService`:
    - `publish` and `publicationProblems` refuse a flagged item;
    - `clearRereview` (see the design calls);
    - `openFlags` returns flags with `raised_at >= rereview_since`, compared as a DATETIME(6) string.
  - Route `POST /intelligence/{item}/rereview/clear`.
  - Item page: a `rereview` prop (banner, flags, clear form); the evidence is named only if `EvidenceLinking::summaries` returns it.
  - Provenance: a `flags` list; flag ULIDs added to the shown set, so audit contexts don't redact them.

## Design calls for review
1. **Separation of duties for clearing.** It needs `intelligence.publish` and the Director role for confidential, restricted or high-impact items. Never the author or owner. The approving reviewer *may* clear.
2. **Clearing guard.** Every non-withdrawn claim must still have a `supports` link to intact, available evidence the director can see. Otherwise the only ways out are retracting or replacing.
3. **What is flagged.** Only approved and published items, and only via claims that are not withdrawn. Every relation counts (supports, contradicts, context). Items still in review aren't flagged: approval and publication already refuse withdrawn or corrupted support.
4. **Withdrawal permission.** Reuses `evidence.hold`. Withdrawal is final, and refused while on legal hold.
5. **No deletion.** O38's deleting job stays for later.
6. **The integrity mark is final.** Restoring the bytes doesn't clear it; a repair path is not built.
7. **Delivery is asynchronous.** The relay runs every minute. Between the withdrawal and the delivery, an approved item could be published if another supporting asset remains; it is flagged on delivery.

## Review focus
1. **Concurrency and locks:**
   - asset→blob order in withdrawal and re-hash vs scan, hold and reconciler;
   - item locks in the handler vs publish, retract, clear and draftReplacement;
   - `intact()` share-locking blobs after assets.
   - Any deadlock, lost flag or flag on the wrong item?
2. **Leakage.** Banner, provenance flags, audit contexts (`evidence_ulid`, `flag_ulid`) and the evidence page (SHA-256 after withdrawal): anything describing evidence or items the viewer can't see?
3. **Integrity check correctness:**
   - `readVersion` on S3 (version required) and on the fake disk;
   - error classification (only NoSuchKey/NoSuchVersion count as missing);
   - streaming of large objects.
4. **Outbox use.** Idempotency under at-least-once delivery; the handler's behaviour if an item is not visible to anyone; the event-definition order across providers (Evidence defines, Intelligence subscribes).
5. **Migration.** Rollback refusals and the CHECK swap on `verification_reviews`.
6. **Tests.** What is missing?
