## Objective
Holders of `grant.manage` give a user or a team access up to a classification, optionally until an expiry; grants are revoked, never deleted; self-grants are blocked; every change is audited (docs/11 P1-14, docs/02 §3.2, docs/07 §3 and T2). PO decision 2026-11-01: only scope `all` until the registry adds competitors and markets; that story adds the typed targets with foreign keys.

New grant class `no_delete` (SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE) in `TableGrants` and `config/database.php`, used for `access_grants`. This changes the least-privilege policy and needs PO approval at merge.

## Review focus
1. Self-grant and escalation (T2): can anyone obtain a grant for themselves, directly, through a team (in either order: grant then join, join then grant, concurrently), or above their own clearance? Can `can_export` be handed out?
2. Revocation and integrity: can a grant be deleted, revoked twice, or changed after revocation? Do the CHECK constraints and the `no_delete` class hold without the service?
3. Grant class change: is `no_delete` applied and verified like the other classes (plan, apply, verify, tests), with no way to widen privileges?
4. Authorization coverage: route middleware and service checks, with two-factor required for `grant.manage` (P1-12); inactive actors and grantees.
5. Expiry and time: UTC storage, `active()` and `heldBy()` correctness (these become the inputs of `AccessScope`, P1-15).
6. Audit and exposure: context fields (no free text), what the page sends to the browser.
