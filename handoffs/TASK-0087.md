## Review focus

2. **Authorization.** Author or owner; `intelligence.submit`; hidden items 404; the problems list shown only to those who may submit. Do the problem messages leak anything?
3. **Concurrency.**

4. **Integrity.** The version stays at submission. Is that consistent with "approvals bind to version + content hash" (P1-43/P1-44)?
5. **Tests.** Does `IntelligenceSubmissionTest` prove the guard? What is missing?
