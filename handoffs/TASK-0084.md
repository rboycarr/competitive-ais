## Objective
P1-40: "As an analyst, I write an intelligence item with typed, rated claims". Acceptance (docs/11): subjects (competitor/product/market); claim types; Admiralty rating; sanitized Markdown.

## Changes
- **New module `modules/Intelligence`:**
  - migration `2026_11_24_000100_create_intelligence_tables` (items, three subject pivots, claims);
  - models;
  - `IntelligenceItemService` and `IntelligenceClaimService`;
  - controllers and routes;
  - pages `Intelligence/Index` and `Intelligence/Show`, and `Components/ItemForm.vue`;
  - contract `IntelligenceReferences`.
- **Visibility:** `IntelligenceItem::seenBy` / `canBeSeenBy` is AccessScope plus statusVisible (docs/07 §3). Until an item is released (published, retracted, superseded), only its author, owner and assigned reviewer, and holders of `intelligence.review`, see it. Hidden items are 404.
- **Edits:**
  - only while the item is editable, and only by its author or owner;
  - the form's `version` must equal the current one, checked under the item's row lock;
  - every change raises the version; saving with nothing changed keeps it.
- **Subjects:**
  - competitors and products the writer can see, plus markets; at least one; at most 20 of each;
  - a product's competitor is added as a competitor subject;
  - archived competitors are refused under a share lock (`CompetitorLookup::lockUnarchived`). Archiving checks for open items under the row lock (`CompetitorService::setLifecycle`).
- **Classification and visibility checks:**
  - the writer must be able to see the resulting item;
  - lowering a classification needs `intelligence.publish`;
  - an item with a subject its editor can no longer see cannot be edited.
- **Claims:**
  - the ratings are never defaulted;
  - at most 50 active claims per item;
  - withdrawn, never deleted;
  - moving swaps a claim with its active neighbour.
- **Markdown:**
  - `Modules/Platform/src/Support/SafeMarkdown.php`: CommonMark through `Str::markdown` with `html_input=escape` and `allow_unsafe_links=false`, then a DOM allowlist of tags and attributes. Images become their alt text; links keep only http(s) or mailto addresses.
  - The output is a node tree, rendered by `resources/js/Components/MarkdownView.ts` without `v-html`.
- **Grants:** items and claims `no_delete`; the subject pivots `mutable`.
- **Audit:** codes, counts and IDs only.

## Review focus
1. **Authorization and visibility.**
   - The statusVisible rule: list, detail and every write path.
   - The 404 versus 403 choices.
   - Subjects hidden from the viewer: counted, never named.
   - Is `ensureSeenBy` re-checked under the lock correctly?
2. **Concurrency.**
   - Version checks under `lockForUpdate`.
   - Claim position swaps.
   - Item creation versus competitor archiving (share lock versus row lock plus the reference check under REPEATABLE READ).
3. **Integrity.**
   - Can content change outside `draft` / `changes_requested`?
   - Can a version bump be skipped, or an approval of an earlier version count later?
   - Subject removal widens a draft's visibility (audited). Is that acceptable?
4. **Sanitizer.** Any bypass of `SafeMarkdown`, or of `MarkdownView`: protocol-relative links, entity tricks, autolinks, nested lists, tables.
5. **Migration.** CHECKs, the `ascii_bin` rating column, FK actions, rollback refusal.
6. **Tests.**
   - Do `IntelligenceItemTest` (including the shared matrix over released items) and `SafeMarkdownTest` prove the acceptance?
   - What is missing?
