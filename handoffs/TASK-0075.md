## Objective
P1-36a (PO decision O37 split P1-36): declared scopes at upload; visibility everywhere follows them (all-of, through AccessScope); matrix rows. Two parts move to P1-36b in Sprint 1.4: inherited scopes on link (with P1-41) and provenance hiding (with P1-45).

## Changes
- **Migration `2026_11_21_000100_create_evidence_scope_tables`:** `evidence_asset_competitor` and `evidence_asset_market`.
  - Composite primary key; `origin` CHECK (`declared`, `inherited`); `added_by`; `created_at`.
  - FK CASCADE on the asset, RESTRICT on the competitor or market.
  - `down()` is refused while any scope exists.
- **`config/database.php`:** both tables are `append_only` (the app account gets SELECT and INSERT only).
- **Models:**
  - New `EvidenceAssetCompetitor` and `EvidenceAssetMarket`.
  - `EvidenceAsset::scopeRelations()` now names the pivots.
  - `competitorIds()` and `marketIds()` read the loaded relation when one is set (the upload's unsaved asset), otherwise they query.
- **`EvidenceUploadService`:**
  - Scopes are declared by ULID through the Registry contracts: `CompetitorLookup::findVisible` (the uploader must see the competitor) and `MarketLookup::find`.
  - At most 20 of each; duplicates are ignored; values are validated before any lookup.
  - The classification check runs first, with no scopes. Then the uploader must see the asset with its scopes, or the upload fails with a `scopes` error.
  - Pivot rows are saved in the asset's transaction (`declared`, `added_by` = the uploader).
- **Controller:**
  - The index offers competitors the viewer can see and all markets (markets are unclassified), to uploaders only.
  - `show` lists the asset's competitors (only names the viewer can see) and markets.
  - The classification probe sets empty scope relations.
- **Request:** `competitors`/`markets` are arrays of 26-character strings.
- **UI:** multi-selects on the upload form, with a warning that scopes narrow visibility and cannot be removed. The detail page lists the scopes.

## Review focus
1. Does every read path (list, detail, download, hold, and any query through `seenBy`/`visibleTo`) now apply the scopes? Is anything still reading evidence without `seenBy`?
2. **Upload:** can a user declare a scope that hides evidence from themselves, or scope it to a competitor they cannot see? Can one probe whether a hidden competitor exists from the error messages?
3. **`competitorIds()` reading a loaded relation:** can a stale or partially loaded relation make `AccessScope::allows()` decide wrongly anywhere else? For example, a model loaded with only some scopes.
4. **Append-only:** is the app account truly unable to remove a scope? Is FK CASCADE on the asset side a risk, given assets are never deleted by the app?
5. **The show page's names:** competitor names are filtered to those the viewer can see. Is there a leak through the market names, or the counts?
