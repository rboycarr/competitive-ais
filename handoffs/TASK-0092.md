## Objective
P1-45: "As an auditor, I trace a published item to its origins". Acceptance (docs/11): a provenance view running claims → evidence → blob hash → source + policy version → custody → reviews → audit, with hidden evidence shown as a placeholder.

## Changes
- **`ProvenanceController`** (`GET /intelligence/{item}/provenance`; route needs `intelligence.view`; an item the viewer can't see is 404):
  - Claims with their citations, as the viewer may see them:
    - evidence through `EvidenceProvenance::traces` (`evidence.view` + `EvidenceAsset::seenBy`);
    - sources through `SourceLookup::recorded` (a placeholder when hidden);
    - hidden evidence is counted per claim and never described.
  - Reviews with their versions and hashes; the current content hash compared with the current version's approval.
  - Retraction, plus links to the replaced and superseding items (only when visible).
  - The audit trail for `audit.view` holders: `AuditEvent::visibleTo`, subject = the item, at most 200 rows. Showing it is audited as `intelligence.provenance_viewed`.
- **Evidence contract** `EvidenceProvenance` / `EvidenceTrace`, implemented by `EloquentEvidenceProvenance`.
- **Intelligence contract** `IntelligenceCitations::forEvidence`, used by `EvidenceController::show`, which now also shows `sha256` and a `citations` prop (null without `intelligence.view`). Items the viewer sees are named; the others are counted.
- **Pages:** `Provenance.vue`, a "Provenance" link on the item page, and a "Cited by" section and SHA-256 on the evidence page.

## Design calls for review
1. The provenance view is open to anyone who can see the item. Only the audit trail needs `audit.view`.
2. The audit trail covers events whose subject is the item. Evidence events appear through each asset's custody trail, not the audit log.
3. Viewing provenance is audited only when the audit trail is shown, matching how audit searches are audited.
4. Excerpts and locators are shown for visible evidence (the item page already shows them).
5. The hidden-evidence count is per claim, and counts each link to an asset the viewer can't see.

## Review focus
1. **Leakage.** Does anything describe evidence, sources, items or audit rows the viewer can't see? Check counts, relations, hashes, custody actor names, linked items, and the JSON of audit contexts (whose values are codes, IDs, ULIDs and counts).
2. **Correctness.** Is the hash comparison right (current version's latest approval vs the recomputed hash)? Can a stale approval show as "matches"?
3. **Module boundaries.** Evidence now depends on an Intelligence contract, and Intelligence on Evidence contracts. Is that cycle acceptable under ADR-0001 (contracts only)?
4. **Performance.** N+1 risks (`SourceLookup::recorded` per citation, user summaries), and the 200-row audit cap.
5. **Tests.** What is missing?
