## Objective
Analysts record a competitor's aliases and its corporate relationships with other competitors (docs/11 P1-21, docs/02 §3.3, docs/03 Registry). Two new tables in grant class `mutable`, with CHECKs for the alias normal form, types, the precision period, no self-relationship and no backwards period. `CompetitorAliasService` normalizes aliases (unique per competitor ignoring case and accents via the collation). `CompetitorRelationshipService` locks both competitor rows in ID order and refuses overlapping ranges for the same pair and type (either direction, except `distributor`). A relationship has no classification of its own: it is shown or changed only for viewers who can see both competitors. Audit events carry IDs and codes only, with the competitor's classification (aliases) or the higher of the two (relationships).

## Review focus
1. Authorization and exposure: can anyone read, add, edit or remove an alias or relationship of a competitor they cannot see, or learn a hidden competitor's name, ULID or existence (profile props, `others`, error messages, 404 vs. validation paths, URL scoping of `{alias}` and `{relationship}`)? Is `competitor.manage` enforced in the services as well as on the routes?
2. Overlap and integrity: is the overlap predicate right (inclusive ends, open ends, edit excluding itself, direction rule)? Are the lock order, the locking read and the UNIQUE backstop sufficient against concurrent writes, and free of deadlocks with `CompetitorService`? (Writers on different pairs can deadlock on gap locks; add and update retry their transaction up to 3 times, `ATTEMPTS`.) Do the CHECKs match the service's normalization (month/year starts, alias normal form)?
3. Audit: right classification, no user input in context, events only on real changes.
4. Grants: `competitor_aliases` and `competitor_relationships` as `mutable` (DELETE allowed for audited corrections). Is that appropriate, or should relationships be `no_delete`?
5. Frontend: no unsafe rendering of alias text, names or notes; the payload mapping of the relationship form.
