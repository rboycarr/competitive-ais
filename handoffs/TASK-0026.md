## Objective
Visibility is decided in one place (ADR-0005, docs/07 §3, docs/11 P1-15): `AccessScope` with `allows()`, `applyTo()` (the `visibleTo` scope) and `ensureVisible()` (404 for hidden records), plus a shared authorization matrix harness in `tests/Support` that every module reuses. Grants have only scope `all` until the registry adds competitors and markets (PO decision 2026-11-01), so a scoped record is covered only by an `all` grant at or above its classification. Binding rule from TASK-0025: inactive accounts see nothing, whatever their grants.

## Review focus
1. Predicate correctness: do `allows()` and `applyTo()` implement docs/07 §3 exactly (clearance, restricted needs a grant, all-of scopes, active and expired/revoked grants, team grants), and do they agree with each other? Any SQL shape (OR precedence, empty IN lists, whereDoesntHave) that widens the result?
2. Default deny: unknown user types, inactive accounts, models that do not implement the contracts, super admins (no bypass).
3. Caching: the per-request grant cache (scoped binding) and `forget()` — can a revoked grant or a removed team membership stay effective, within a request, across requests, or in queue workers?
4. Module boundaries: contracts in Platform, implementation in Identity; nothing else decides visibility.
5. The matrix harness: is its oracle independent of the implementation and strong enough to catch a wrong predicate? What is missing before modules depend on it?
