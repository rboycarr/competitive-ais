## Objective
Users see where they are signed in and sign out other devices; every revocation is audited (docs/11 P1-17, docs/07 T6 "session revoke"). PO decision 2026-11-01: sessions move from Valkey to the MySQL `sessions` table (`SESSION_DRIVER=database`) so they can be listed per user. Session IDs are bearer secrets: the page identifies sessions by an HMAC handle. Revoking deletes the session row and cycles the remember token (otherwise the revoked device's "keep me signed in" cookie would sign it back in).

## Review focus
1. Secrecy: can a session ID (own or another user's) reach the browser, logs or the audit trail? Is the handle safe (keyed, constant-time comparison)?
2. Authorization: can a user list or end another user's session, or their current one? Password confirmation and the enrollment allowlist (`sessions.destroy`, `sessions.destroy-others` reachable before MFA enrollment).
3. Effectiveness: after revocation, can the device continue (remember cookie, cached session, concurrent request re-saving the session row)?
4. Driver change: risks of moving sessions to MySQL (grants, the sessions table, payload contents, CSRF/session fixation, performance), and what operations must know.
5. Audit: `session.revoked` context and subject.
