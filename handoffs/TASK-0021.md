## Objective
Privileged permissions (`user.manage`, `role.manage`, `grant.manage`, `intelligence.publish`, `source.approve`, `export.run`) work only after two-factor authentication is confirmed; users holding one are sent to enroll and can do nothing else meanwhile (docs/03 Identity rules, docs/09 #2: a test per permission).

## Review focus
1. Can a privileged permission be exercised without confirmed two-factor: any path around `User::hasPermissionTo` (spatie's `hasDirectPermission`, `hasAnyPermission`, `getAllPermissions`, `@can`, wildcard permissions, super-user patterns, Gate::before order)?
2. Can a user who must enroll reach anything but enrollment and sign-out (path allowlist, JSON, API routes, prefix tricks such as `user/security-anything`)?
3. Can enrollment be bypassed by a pending (unconfirmed) secret, or by disabling two-factor afterwards?
4. Shared props and the security page: anything exposed?
5. Is deriving the requirement from permissions (instead of the ERD's `mfa_required` column) sound?
