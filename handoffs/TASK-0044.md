## Objective
Grant access to the records about one market (docs/11 P1-23c, docs/02 §3.2 "Market grants", docs/07 §3; PO decision O28), built the same way as competitor grants (P1-20a).
- **Database:** `access_grants.market_id`, a foreign key to markets with RESTRICT. The scope CHECK is widened so each scope names exactly its target: `all` neither, `competitor` only a competitor, `market` only a market. Rollback refuses while market grants exist.
- **`GrantSet`:** gains `markets` and `marketsCovering()`. `covers()` is all-of across competitors and markets together.
- **`GrantAccessScope`:**
  - `applyTo()`: per readable level, no competitor and no market outside the covered sets; a restricted record needs at least one scope.
  - `effectiveGrants()`: reads market grants.
- **`AccessGrantService`:** a grant opens one competitor (visible to the actor) or one market (any existing market, through Registry's new `MarketLookup` contract), never both. Audit context gains `market`.
- **Admin › Access grants:** a "records about one market" option; the grants list shows "code · name".
- **Authorization matrix:**
  - real markets M and N;
  - grant variants: market M at confidential, market N at restricted, and the pair "competitor A plus market M" at confidential;
  - scope shapes: market M, market N, markets M+N, competitor A + market M.

## Review focus
1. **Visibility correctness:**
   - Do `allows()` and `applyTo()` agree with the docs/07 predicate in every case? Consider records with both scope types, several markets, restricted records with only market scopes, and grants at different levels for the competitor and the market.
   - Can a market grant ever widen access to something unscoped or about another market?
   - Is the matrix's `expected()` an independent statement of the predicate (not a copy of the code)?
2. **Database:** is the CHECK exhaustive? Is the rollback guard safe (checked before any DDL)? Is the migration order safe against the markets table?
3. **Authorization:**
   - only `grant.manage`;
   - no granting to oneself or one's teams;
   - clearance ceiling;
   - the "both targets" refusal.
   Is it acceptable that any existing market can be named (markets are unclassified)?
4. **Module boundaries:** Identity uses only Registry contracts.
5. **Audit and UI:** context without user input; market names shown only as code and name.
