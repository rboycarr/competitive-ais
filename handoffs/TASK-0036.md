## Objective
Brands (ours or a competitor's) and the product category tree (docs/11 P1-22a, docs/02 §3.3, docs/03 Registry; PO decisions O25, O26). `brands`: NULL-safe unique key per owner, registry name normal form (shared with aliases), visible only with the owning competitor visible; `BrandService` needs `product.manage`. `product_categories`: ancestor-ID paths, at most six levels, CHECKs tying path, depth and parent together; `CategoryService` needs `taxonomy.manage`, locks the parent row on create and every category row on move, rewrites the subtree in one statement, and removes only empty categories. Both tables `mutable`. New Brands page and Admin › Categories; navigation flags `productView`, `taxonomyManage`.

## Review focus
1. Exposure: can anyone see, change or learn the existence of a brand whose competitor they cannot see (index props, `competitors` options, error messages, 404 vs. validation)? Is `scopeVisibleTo` on `Brand` correct, including inactive users and restricted competitors?
2. Tree integrity: can the tree get a cycle, an inconsistent path/depth, more than six levels, or an orphan, through concurrent creates, moves and removals, or through the subtree `UPDATE` (prefix replacement, `SUBSTRING` offset, `CAST(depth AS SIGNED)`, the ascii collation conversion)? Do the CHECKs match what the service writes?
3. Authorization: `product.manage` / `taxonomy.manage` on routes and in services; super admins (`taxonomy.manage` without `product.view`).
4. Audit: classification (competitor's for its brands, internal otherwise), context without user input, no events for no-ops.
5. Frontend: text rendering, payload mapping (`parent: null` for the top level), the move options excluding the category and its subtree.
